## Project Overview
Main Focus is a clothing brand for new to young boys
WEDE5020 Part 1 is mainly focuses on creating a website using HTML.

## Website goals and Objectives
## Primary Goal 
To help parents find parenthood much easier when it comes to boys, by giving them lots of options for choosing clothes. It’s here to support not just the mothers, but also the boys by giving them stylish clothes and helping them make memories with those clothes. 
## Key Objectives:
•	Sell Products Easily: Make it simple for parents to click, choose, and buy outfits for newborns up to young kids—whether they need something for a wedding, church, or just everyday vacation styling.
•	Grow Website Traffic: Reach more parents out there who are tired of struggling to find good boys' clothes, helping them easily discover and use our platform.
•	Share Clear Information: Give parents straightforward details on sizing, strong materials, and prices so they never have to guess or feel overwhelmed when shopping.
•	Keep Parents Connected: Collect inquiries and sign-ups so we can stay close to our community and always be a trusted destination for moms and dads.
Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
To check if the website is actually doing its job right:
•	Sales Conversion Rate: Looking at how many people visit the site and actually end up buying clothes.
•	Visitor Traffic: Tracking how many parents are landing on the pages and checking out our product categories.
•	Bounce Rate: Seeing if people find what they need right away or if they are leaving too quickly, so we know how to keep things welcoming.
•	Total Orders: Keeping track of how many purchases come through and which items parents love the most.

## Key Features and Functionality
•	UI/UX Design: The website is designed to be very simple, human, welcoming, and comforting to help ease overwhelmed parents. When I say overwhelming, I don't mean that people don't deserve to be parents—they do! It's just that things can get overwhelming when you can't find what you are supposed to be finding. The design ensures everything is clear and easy to navigate.
•	Product Catalog: Main Focus offers a very affordable catalog with very strong quality, supporting boys from newborns up to young kids. It features clothing styles for every occasion, whether you are dressing them up for church, a wedding, or just styling them to go on vacation.
•	Core Features: Quick-access navigation and stress-free pathways designed to make shopping easy for busy parents.
•	CMS Backend: A reliable content management system that allows easy updates to products, categories, and site details.
